about=Connects QGIS to a SMART Connect server (SMART conservation software). Sign in with your SMART Connect account, choose one of your saved queries and a date range, and the results are added to the map: patrol tracks as lines, observations as points, grid queries as polygons and summary queries as tables. Downloads are saved locally so layers keep working offline. Requires a SMART Connect account with access to saved queries. Not an official SMART product; SMART and the SMART logo belong to the SMART Partnership.
